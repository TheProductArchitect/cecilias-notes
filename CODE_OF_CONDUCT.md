# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Short version: be kind, be specific, assume good faith.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for users of the app and the long-term
  health of the codebase.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Personal or political attacks, harassment, or insults.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit
  permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
  in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the
repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any
private channels used to coordinate project work. It also applies
when an individual is officially representing the project in public
spaces.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behaviour go privately to **nvg1996@gmail.com**.
All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly. Reporters'
identities are kept confidential to the extent possible.

The maintainer ([@TheProductArchitect](https://github.com/TheProductArchitect))
is responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour
and is expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any instance of unacceptable behaviour.

Consequences for violations may include:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was wrong and
   why.
2. **Warning** — a public note plus a request to stop the behaviour
   and reflect.
3. **Temporary ban** — removal of interaction privileges for a
   defined period.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal of all interaction privileges.

The maintainer's decision is final.
